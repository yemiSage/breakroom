Privacy notice

UPDATED 6 NOVEMBER 2026

Breakroom is a live quiz app. You can host or join a game as a guest, or choose to sign in.

Account information

Email sign-in uses your email address and a one-time code. Google sign-in receives your Google account identifier, name, email address, email verification status, and profile image when Google provides them. Breakroom uses this information to create your account, sign you in, and suggest your display name when joining a quiz.

Account and session records are stored on Cloudflare. Session records include your IP address and browser information. Sign-in cookies last up to seven days and are renewed during use. Account records remain until the operator removes them. Signing out ends your current session.

Email and service providers

Brevo receives your email address and the login message so it can deliver your code. Cloudflare hosts the Online app and stores account and Online game data. Google processes the Google sign-in request when you choose that option. If a host uses AI rephrasing, the selected question and answer options are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI.

Quiz information

Games use a display name, submitted answers, and scores. The host can see results and download saved Local game records. Active rooms expire after 24 hours. Saved results can remain on the host laptop or in Cloudflare storage until the operator removes them. Local game uploads depend on the host's server configuration.
